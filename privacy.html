Papa's Paper

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Papa's Paper turns a grandparent's spoken stories into a small newspaper that their family gets by email. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and how to have it deleted. We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our database provider.
  • Your profile: the name your family calls you, the pronouns the paper uses, the language you speak in, and your phone's time zone (so the paper arrives in the evening your time).
  • Your stories: the voice recordings you make in the app, written transcripts of them, and any photos you choose to go with a story. Stories you mark “keep it private” are stored with your account but are never put in a paper or sent to anyone.
  • Your family list: the names and email addresses of the people you add to receive your paper.
  • Family responses: the emoji reactions and written replies family members send from the paper, and their name as you entered it.

The app also keeps copies of your recordings, photos and papers on your phone so it works without internet.

How we use it

  • To ask your permission first: before your first story, the app explains where your recordings and photos go and asks you to agree.
  • To have a conversation with you: your recordings are turned into text, and the text is used to choose a follow-up question.
  • To write and email your paper each evening to the family members you added, using the stories you chose to include.
  • To show you your past papers and your family's reactions and replies in the app.
  • To let you sign back in on a new phone and get your paper back, and to email you a link if you forget your password.

We don't use your stories for advertising, and we don't share them with anyone except the services below and the family members you choose.

Services that process your information

To provide the app, we send information to these companies, which process it on our behalf:

  • Supabase stores your account, stories, recordings, photos, family list and papers.
  • OpenAI turns your voice recordings into text and reads the app's questions aloud in a natural voice.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads your transcripts to choose follow-up questions, and reads the stories and photos you included to write each evening's paper.
  • Resend delivers the paper and welcome emails to your family, and password-reset emails to you.

Under their terms for business customers, OpenAI and Anthropic don't use information sent through their APIs to train their models.

What your family sees

Family members you add receive the stories you marked “include,” written up as a newspaper, along with any photos you chose. Photos in the email are shown through private links that only work for people who have the email. Each family member's email contains their own private links for reacting, replying and unsubscribing. Family members can unsubscribe at any time from any paper.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account, so your past papers stay available to you. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, recordings, transcripts, photos, family list, papers, and your family's reactions and replies.

Deleting your account or information

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, right in the app: open Family and tap Delete my paper. This permanently deletes your account, profile, recordings, transcripts, photos, family list and past papers, along with your family's reactions and replies, from our servers and from your phone.

To delete a single story, or if you can't get into the app, email support@papaspaper.com from the email address you signed up with and we'll take care of it, usually within a few days. You can also remove any family member from your list in the app at any time.

Children

Papa's Paper is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Your sign-in is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, and your stories and photos are kept in private storage that is not publicly accessible.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions? Email support@papaspaper.com.
