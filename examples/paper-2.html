The Night the Power Went Out on Christmas Eve

“Nobody complained. Nobody dared.”

In 1971, an ice storm took out the power on Christmas Eve with a twenty-pound turkey half cooked.

Nana finished it in the fireplace, a piece at a time, in her mother's cast iron pan. "Nobody complained," she said. "Nobody dared."

The family ate by candlelight on the living room floor. It is, she says, still the best Christmas she can remember.
