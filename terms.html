The free app

Papa's Paper is free to download and use. On the free plan, each evening's paper goes to up to 5 people on your list. You can record stories, fix their words, choose what's private, and save your papers to your phone.

Papa's Paper+

  • Papa's Paper+ is an auto-renewing subscription: $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year in the US (prices vary by country and are shown in the app before you buy).
  • It adds unlimited people on your list, putting the pieces of a story in your own order, and sharing any paper with anyone.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
  • If Papa's Paper+ ends, nobody is removed from your list. The paper keeps going to the first 5 people you added, and the rest are paused until you renew.

Your stories

Your stories, recordings and photos belong to you. You give us permission to process them only to make and send your paper, as described in the Privacy Policy. Please only add people who'd like to get your paper, and don't share things you don't have the right to share.

The AI

Papers are written with the help of AI from your own words. It can make mistakes, so you can preview and fix your paper before it goes out.
