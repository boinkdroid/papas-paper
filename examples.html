This is what lands in the family’s inbox

On any evening there’s a story to tell, the family gets an edition like these, written from what Grandma or Grandpa said that day.

Nana’s PaperTo: the whole family · 6:00 PM

What to notice

  • Their own words. The stories are written from what they actually said, quotes and all.
  • How they’re doing. A short column about their day: who they saw, what they did, how they’re feeling.
  • One-tap reactions under every story.
  • Write back. Replies show up on their phone, under My papers.
