Help & Support

Need a hand? Email catiecorley@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

How it works

Open the app and tap Answer a question or Tell a story. Talk as long as you like, then tap I'm finished. The app may ask a few follow-up questions. At the end, choose Yes, include it to put the story in tonight's paper, or No, keep it private.

At 6 PM your time, everyone on your family list gets a newspaper by email with the stories you included that day. If you didn't include any stories, no paper goes out.

Common questions

My family isn't getting the paper. Check that they're listed under Family in the app with the right email address, and ask them to look in their spam or promotions folder. A paper only goes out on days you include a story.

The app says to allow the microphone. Open the iPhone Settings app, scroll down to Papa's Paper, and turn on Microphone.

I got a new phone. Install Papa's Paper, tap I already have a paper, and sign in with the same email and password. Your name, family list and past papers come back.

How does my family write back? Every story in the email has reaction buttons and a Write back link. Their replies show up in the app under My papers.

A family member wants to stop getting the paper. They can tap Unsubscribe at the bottom of any paper, or you can swipe their name to remove it under Family.

I want to delete my account. Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your account and all your stories. See the privacy policy for details.
